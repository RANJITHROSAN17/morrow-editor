Know where your text goes.

Your nearby text is sent to Ollama on this computer. Ollama can also route requests to cloud models; a local address alone does not guarantee on-device processing. Check your model and Ollama configuration. Suggestions turn off when Morrow restarts.

01

Install and start Ollama on your PC.

02

Download a code model in your terminal:

ollama pull qwen2.5-coder:1.5b
03

Turn on suggestions above. Press Tab to accept, Esc to dismiss.

Model download is separate (about 1 GB for the suggested model). Performance depends on your PC.

    OPTIONAL CLOUD TOOLS
    Arena & change review
    
  
    Choose what to share. Morrow never signs into the Arena website or sends your files automatically.
    
  
    Use Arena in your browser
    Sign in normally, copy only the text you choose, and paste a response into Morrow for review.
    
  
  
    Use the Arena API optional · separate from website login
    Requires an eligible Arena API key and model. API access, costs, rate limits and privacy terms depend on your account; free website access or a free-router key may not work here. Selected text and your instruction are sent to Arena and its model providers.
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    Morrow also sends a fixed instruction asking for replacement text. It does not add the file path or other files, but your selected text or instruction may contain them. Review both above before each request. The local credential warning is incomplete and can be mistaken; always check manually.
