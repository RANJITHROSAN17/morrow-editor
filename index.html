Know where your text goes.

Your nearby text is sent to Ollama on this computer. Ollama can also route requests to cloud models; a local address alone does not guarantee on-device processing. Check your model and Ollama configuration. Suggestions turn off when Morrow restarts.

01

Install and start Ollama on your PC.

02

Download a code model in your terminal:

ollama pull qwen2.5-coder:1.5b
03

Turn on suggestions above. Press Tab to accept, Esc to dismiss.

Model download is separate (about 1 GB for the suggested model). Performance depends on your PC.
