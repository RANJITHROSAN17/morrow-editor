Your nearby text is sent to Ollama on this computer. Choose a local model to keep it on your device. Suggestions turn off when Morrow restarts; enable them again when needed.

01

Install and start Ollama on your PC.

02

Download a code model in your terminal:

ollama pull qwen2.5-coder:1.5b
03

Turn on suggestions above. Press Tab to accept, Esc to dismiss.

Model download is separate (about 1 GB for the suggested model). Performance depends on your PC.
